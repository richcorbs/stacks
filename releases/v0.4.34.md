## Stacks v0.4.34

### Fixes

- **Reliable background notifications** — activity notifications now wait for macOS permission before delivery, and background delivery failures no longer interrupt work with an error toast.
- **Multiline Pi prompt history** — recalled multiline prompts no longer trap keyboard navigation. Use Down at the end to move forward or restore the draft, Up at the beginning to move backward, or Escape to leave history and restore the draft.
