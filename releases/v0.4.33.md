## Stacks v0.4.33

### New

- **Workspace templates** — save reusable New Workspace defaults for workspace name, first pane type, one-time setup command, terminal startup command, and initial split layout.
- **Template-driven creation** — choose a saved template from Cmd-P to open the New Workspace dialog with its values pre-populated, then review or adjust them before creating the workspace.
- **Template management** — add, edit, and delete workspace templates directly from Cmd-P.
